---
name: Post-close note audience
description: Confirmed business policy for ticket-note visibility after closure.
---

New notes added to a terminal ticket are visible only to admin-panel users who can access that ticket. Technicians and 1099 providers must not see or add those new notes. Historical notes remain shared rather than being reclassified.

**Why:** The user confirmed that post-close notes are internal staff notes, while explicitly requiring preservation of existing shared history. They also chose to retain broad VRS-agent access for non-queued tickets.

**How to apply:** Treat terminal-ticket notes as staff-only at write time. Keep existing division rules for queued tickets and current cross-division access for non-queued tickets.
