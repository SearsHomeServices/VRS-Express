---
name: Executive reporting definitions
description: Executive email conventions differ intentionally from operational analytics.
---

Executive reports use ticket outcomes, not Stage-1 analytics: completed is the approved executive bucket, rejected includes rejected_closed, and pending includes queued. A literal approved ticket status is reported separately as Other, not silently equated to completed.

**Why:** The requested executive summaries use completed tickets, and Stage-1-based totals materially differed from the previously shared executive figures. Do not change operational statuses to reconcile reporting.

**How to apply:** Keep executive definitions independent of operational analytics. Prior-week comparisons select matching created-at cohorts but observe current outcomes; never describe them as historical point-in-time outcomes without an actual historical snapshot.

The pre-VRS Express process was via phone, not a previous version of this app.

**Why:** The user corrected an executive summary that could imply app-period comparisons measured improvement over the old process.

**How to apply:** Use phone-process baseline data for before/after impact claims. App week-over-week comparisons are not that baseline. Elapsed creation-to-review time is not active labor time; do not convert it directly into dollar savings.

Field-technician labor capacity is valued at $29 per hour.

**Why:** The user supplied this rate for the VRS Express business-impact estimate.

**How to apply:** Apply this rate only to field-technician time saved, not reviewer time. Keep assumed minutes saved distinct from measured savings.

The user supplied an average of 15 minutes per phone call/touchpoint for the field-technician productivity comparison.

**Why:** The requested leadership narrative focuses on the dollar value of technician and employee time freed up.

**How to apply:** At $29/hour, a 15-minute technician call represents $7.25 in capacity. Count avoided calls and subtract replacement submission/follow-up effort before claiming net savings; ticket or message counts alone do not establish calls avoided.

The user estimates $3 million in annual replacement savings for the internal warranty program from better human-in-the-loop review plus AI-first triaging, in addition to technician productivity value.

**Why:** The user supplied this business-impact estimate for leadership reporting.

**How to apply:** Attribute it as a user-provided estimate, not an independently validated result. Distinguish replacement-cost savings from labor capacity value, and disclose assumptions when combining them.
