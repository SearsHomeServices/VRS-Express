The VRS Prescreen CRM continues to support a previously phone-based coordination process through a shared queue, assigned ownership, and traceable service-order outcomes.

This week to date:
- **5,932 tickets entered the CRM.**

## 5. Photo-Review Workload and Staffing

Photo submissions during the four complete weekdays, October 5–8, averaged **126 SO identifiers per day**.

For review-only staffing, use **six minutes per ticket** to examine photos and proposed parts, select the disposition, and document the reason.

| Capacity Measure | One Agent | Two Agents |
|---|---:|---:|
| Productive hours/day: 8-hour shift less 1-hour lunch | **7** | **14** |
| Reviews/day at six minutes each | **70** | **140** |
| Productive hours per five-day week | **35** | **70** |
| Reviews per five-day week | **350** | **700** |

At the recent **126-photo-SO daily volume**, estimated review work is **12.6 hours/day**, or approximately **90% of two agents’ available capacity**.

**Recommendation:** Two weekday reviewers with shared backup, plus weekend coverage if same-day review is required.

This assumes all other work is automated, one review per photo-bearing SO, and all non-lunch time is available for reviews. Photos do not necessarily mean a ticket is complete or eligible for review. Six minutes remains a planning estimate; available logs have not established active review time.
